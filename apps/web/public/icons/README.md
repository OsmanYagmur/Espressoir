Home-screen icons: paper with the red square of the favicon. Plain PNGs, so they are served
as files and cost nothing to deliver. Made once with Pillow (see the 3E commit); to change
them, draw them again at the same sizes: 192 and 512 (square at 56 %), maskable 512 (square
at 42 %, inside the middle 80 % that a launcher may keep), and `src/app/apple-icon.png` (180).
